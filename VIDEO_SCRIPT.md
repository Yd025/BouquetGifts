# Bouquet: demo video script (about 2:00)

**0:00–0:15 · Hook (face cam or title card)**
"Every group gift has one person who guesses the gift, fronts the money, and chases Venmo for weeks. Everyone else knows something that would have made the gift better, and none of it gets used. We built Bouquet."

**0:15–0:30 · Plant** *(screen: landing, with the sample bouquet's real product photos → sign up → Plant a bouquet)*
"I'm making a bouquet for Maya's birthday. It needs to arrive by the 30th, and shipping takes three days, so Bouquet sets a buy-by deadline. If we miss it, nobody gets charged."

**0:30–0:50 · Invite + Card** *(screen: invite link → join page with the T-Mobile badge → questionnaire)*
"Friends tap a face-down card and join with just a name and a number. T-Mobile's network verifies it, so there are no codes and no account yet. Then it's one card per screen. This question was drawn from the deck, so not everyone gets it, and the flower grows as Jon answers. He writes a memory in his own words, mints, and the pack tears open: *The Well-Read Sunrise Hiker*."

**0:50–1:10 · Draw, keep, fuse** *(screen: deck → draw flips → swipe right KEEP stamp → swipe left to discard → hand fan → select two → fusion animation)*
"Everyone's cards flip together, each with its own title and memory. Their answers become a deck. I draw. Film Shooter, legendary, because all three friends said it. Swipe right to keep. Coffee Snob, swipe left. Oops, undo. Now I fuse two cards from my hand, and out comes a trail film camera set. It's a rare fusion, so it only unlocks because two or more friends agreed. My fusion is my vote, and the gift table updates live for everyone. Every gift on the table, even the safe pick, is real products with real photos. That runs on a Cloudflare Durable Object, one per bouquet."

**1:10–1:35 · Bloom + Checkout** *(screen: gift strip lighting up, bloom growing, hold on Visa, "your share" line, checkout → header reads "✓ Purchased in time")*
"Everyone picks their own budget. Jon puts in fifty, Priya forty. Those are Visa holds, not charges, and Cloudflare Turnstile quietly checks it's a human first. As the group fills it, the gift grows into real products from real stores: a 35mm camera, then Kodak Gold film, then a Polaroid. These are their actual photos and prices, and each one lights up at the top of the page as we fund it. Before anyone checks out, Sam can see exactly what they'll pay: twenty-two dollars of their forty-five dollar hold, with the rest going back to their card. At checkout, Bouquet captures the highest tier we fully funded, splits it fairly, and releases the rest. The countdown turns into a green 'Purchased in time.'"

*Honesty note (don't read aloud): until the Visa sandbox keys arrive at 3 PM and are wired in, holds run on our mock payments service. If a judge asks, say so.*

**1:35–1:55 · Reveal** *(screen: recipient pack → cards flip with voice → garland gift)*
"Maya tears open her pack. Every card turns a trait into a compliment, 'You chase every sunrise', and a Gemini voice reads that friend's memory in their own words. The last card is legendary: her gift, with a photo of exactly what's coming, in a garland, with confetti."

*(optional, 10s, judge drawer: click the small 🎬 button at the bottom right)* "And for the judges: a SIM-swapped phone tries to put $60 on hold, and T-Mobile blocks it. Fast-forward past the deadline, and every hold is released."

**1:55–2:00 · Close**
"Bouquet. Gifts grown by everyone who loves them. Built on Visa, T-Mobile, and Cloudflare, with Google Gemini."

*Recording tip (don't read aloud): record at 1280px or wider so the 🎬 button stays in the page margin. For a phone cut-in, the room works at 390px: the friend cards swipe sideways in one row.*
