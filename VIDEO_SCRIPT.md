*Recording order (don't read aloud): Reset demo puts Maya's bouquet at the card stage. Record 0:35–1:15 first (add your card, make gift ideas, pick the gift), then the cold open on Jon's phone once the bouquet is funding, then 1:15 onward. Edit the cold open to the front.*

**0:00–0:20 · Cold open: the conditional voice pledge** *(screen, phone frame: Maya's bouquet, funding. Tap 🎙)*
Jon taps the mic and says: *"Thirty now, forty-five if we get the [last item on the gift strip]."* (Use the name of the last item on screen, e.g. "the record".)
"Payment forms want numbers. People talk in conditions. Bouquet hears both. If the phrase fits two items on the table, it asks which one, one tap, instead of guessing. Then it's three numbers you can read in three seconds: thirty now, forty-five max, and one line: *'$45 max, only if it's the [item].'* One tap on 'Looks right: hold $30' and the card sheet is filled in with thirty dollars. The extra fifteen waits, and is only offered if the group goes for that item."

**0:20–0:35 · Hook**
"Every group gift has one person who guesses the gift, fronts the money and chases Venmo for weeks. Everyone else knows something that would have made it better. We built Bouquet: the gift is grown by everyone who loves her, and nobody fronts the money."

**0:35–0:55 · Start + Card** *(screen: Start a gift → invite card → questionnaire → pack tears open)*
"Maya's gift needs to arrive by the 30th, and shipping takes three days, so Bouquet sets a buy-by deadline. Miss it, and nobody is charged. Friends join with a name and a number, no account. One card per screen; this one is a bonus question, so not everyone gets it. Jon writes a memory, taps 'Finish my card', and the pack tears open: *The Well-Read Sunrise Hiker*."

**0:55–1:15 · Keep, pass, make a gift** *(screen: deck → flick keep/pass → hand → 'Make a gift' → Gift ideas; 'Not sure? Get a suggestion')*
"Everyone's answers become a deck. Flick right to keep, left to pass. Put two cards together and out comes a gift idea. Rare finds only unlock when two friends agree, and the gift ideas and votes update live for everyone on a Cloudflare Durable Object. Stuck? Tap for a suggestion: Gemini says what sets each gift apart and names whose cards it uses, without ever adding up anyone's private budget."

**1:15–1:45 · The money, readable in three seconds** *(screen: Priya's phone opens the invite → one screen: gift photo, $15/$25/$40, 'Chip in $25'; then the card sheet's 'Hold $25'; then the one-tap top-up; then 'What will I pay?')*
"Priya opens the link late. She gets one screen: the gift, how much is in, three quick amounts, her name and 'Chip in $25', then 'Hold $25' on the card sheet. It's a hold, not a charge, and the line underneath says the most she'll pay and when it drops off. No signup. Adding her own card comes after, and she can skip it.
The group is a few dollars short of the next item. Bouquet splits that gap fairly, in proportion to the room each friend has left under their own limit, and each one sees only their own ask: *'Top up $12?'*, *'$12 more unlocks the Record for Maya'*, and *'+$12 from you · 2 other friends are getting their own ask'*. One tap: a SIM-swap check on her number runs first, 'phone check passed', and the next item unlocks for everyone, with a little burst of petals.
And anyone can tap 'What will I pay?': a receipt slip. What you'd be charged, what goes back to your card, and why. Our rules compute every number from the ledger. Gemini only words it, and its version is shown only if every dollar matches."

**1:45–2:00 · Checkout** *(screen: the vine grows and the items light up → 'Ready to buy.' preview with the fee line → 'Buy the gift' → "✓ Bought in time", the stamped ticket and the 'Demo · no real money moved' chip)*
"Before checkout, everyone sees their exact share. At checkout, Bouquet captures the biggest version of the gift we fully funded, on our Visa sandbox,, splits it in proportion to each hold, and releases the rest. Changed your mind? Tap 'Take my money back' any time before it's bought. The countdown turns into 'Bought in time.'"

**2:00–2:10 · Reveal** *(screen: Maya's pack → cards flip with voice → garland)*
"Maya tears open her pack. Each card turns a trait into a compliment, a Gemini voice reads that friend's memory in their own words, and the last card is her gift, in a garland, with confetti."

"Bouquet Gifts. AI that speaks human between your friends and the payment, and rules that keep every dollar exact. Built with Google Gemini, on a Visa sandbox, carrier network APIs and Cloudflare."
