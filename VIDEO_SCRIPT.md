# Bouquet Gifts: demo video script (3:00)

*Setup (not read aloud): follow DEMO_RUNBOOK.md. Phone = Jon (jon@bouquet.gift), laptop = Adelin, the organizer (demo@bouquet.gift), on the seeded demo gift; all passwords bouquet123. Don't record a fresh gift, and don't open the Demo tools drawer on camera. The chat clip is `gigi-chat/gigi-chat.html` (press Space to start, about 18 s) or the backup `gigi-chat.mp4`. Its friends (Jon, Priya, Sam, Leo) match the demo gift.*

**0:00–0:04 · Cold open** *(Adelin on camera, casual, maybe looking at her phone)*
"Oh, it's Gigi's birthday soon. I should get her a gift."

**0:04–0:22 · The group chat** *(cut to the chat clip, full screen, no voiceover. It ends on "link in a sec 🌷")*

**0:22–0:40 · Why** *(Adelin to camera)*
"Every group gift goes like that. Someone guesses the gift, someone fronts the money and chases Venmo for weeks, and the friend on a tight budget has to say 'I can't' in front of everyone. So I built Bouquet, where nobody fronts the money and nobody sees what anyone else gives."

**0:40–0:55 · Starting a bouquet** *(laptop: the organizer's gift page, the share link, the buy-by date)*
"I start a bouquet, set a buy-by date that leaves time for shipping, and drop the link in the chat. Friends join from their phones in one screen, no app to download."

**0:55–1:20 · The cards** *(phone: the quiz, one question per screen, then the finished trait card with its flower back)*
"Each friend answers a few questions about her. Some are shared, some are random, so no two cards are alike. Your answers turn into a trait card with its own title and your own flower on the back. There's even a question for what would annoy her, so bad gifts quietly drop out."

**1:20–1:45 · The deck** *(laptop: draw, keep or pass, put two cards together, the ideas turn over, rare card glow, vote, go with the top pick)*
"Everyone's cards go into one deck. Keep or pass, put two cards together, and a real gift turns over: a hiker plus a coffee snob makes a summit pour-over kit. When friends agree, cards turn rare. These are real products from real shops, and the gift you make is your vote."

**1:45–2:10 · Chipping in** *(phone: tap 🎙 next to "Just say it", then the read-back, the Visa sheet and the vine growing on the laptop)*
Jon says: *"Thirty now, forty-five if we get the [last item on the gift strip]."* (Say the item name shown on screen.)
"People talk in conditions, not form fields. Bouquet reads it back, and one tap holds thirty dollars on his Visa card. A hold, not a charge. As more friends chip in, the gift grows, and each new item opens like a flower on the vine. Nobody can see what Jon put in, only that the gift is growing."

**2:10–2:22 · Trust** *(Leo's phone: chip in $60, then the red block)*
"Leo's number just moved to a new SIM, which is a classic sign of a stolen phone. Bouquet checks with the carrier first and doesn't place the hold, so his card stays safe."

**2:22–2:42 · Bought** *(laptop: Buy the gift, then the Bought ticket and receipt)*
"At checkout, Bouquet charges only what the gift costs, splits it fairly by what everyone offered, and releases the rest back to their cards. Each store gets paid with a one-time Visa card, for exactly that amount, at that store, once." (Read the first receipt line on screen.)

**2:42–3:00 · The reveal** *(the recipient's link: the pack tears open, cards flip, a memory is read aloud, the garland frame)*
"Then she opens a pack of cards from her friends, hears their memories, and finds the gift they grew together. Bouquet: group gifts where everyone chips in their way."

*Mocks, if asked: holds, captures and the one-time Visa cards run on our Visa sandbox. The SIM check uses a real Nokia Network as Code client, but the live call fails on this deployment, so it falls back to a simulator that gives the same answer for Nokia's test numbers. Number Verify is mocked.*
